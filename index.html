FAQ

Questions patients ask

What patients ask before their trip.

Is it safe to get dental work in Tijuana?

Our dentists are licensed in Mexico, use the same implant brands as U.S. clinics, and follow strict sterilization standards.

How many trips will I need?

Usually two: one for the implants and temporary teeth, and one a few months later for your final teeth.

How long does the treatment take?

Your implants and temporary teeth are placed in one visit of a few days. Your final teeth are placed after the healing period, usually 3 to 6 months later.

Can I talk to the dentist before I travel?

Yes. Your free virtual consult is with a specialist, by video call or WhatsApp, before you book anything.

Do you accept U.S. insurance?

We accept many PPO plans and help you with the paperwork. Ask us on WhatsApp.

What if I have a problem after I go home?

Your treatment includes a written warranty, and our team answers on WhatsApp.
